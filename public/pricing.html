Frequently asked
How exactly are credits consumed? ▼
Credits are charged per minute of audio after silence is stripped: 1 credit per minute for a standard MOC, or 2 credits per minute with speaker diarization (a speaker-wise transcript showing who said what). There is no separate charge for MOC generation. A 60-minute conversation uses 60 credits, or 120 with diarization. Free trial minutes are used first. Long pauses do not count.
Do credits expire? ▼
No. Credits are permanent and carry over indefinitely. There is no subscription or renewal.
What if I run out of credits mid-upload? ▼
We check your balance before starting. If you have insufficient credits the upload is blocked before any API cost is incurred. Top up and retry.
What languages are supported? ▼
English, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Punjabi, Malayalam, and multilingual (mixed-language) recordings. The system auto-detects if you set Language to Auto.
Is there a free trial? ▼
New accounts receive 60 minutes of free transcription to evaluate the product. Once the trial is used, a credit pack is required to continue.
Is payment secure? ▼
Payments are processed by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateway. AiX Track never stores your card details. The signature is verified server-side before any credits are added.
