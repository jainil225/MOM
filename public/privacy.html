The Service is hosted in an India region, and recordings, transcripts and minutes are stored there. We do not transfer data to any country restricted by the Government of India under the DPDP Act.

07

Security

We use HTTPS encryption in transit, encryption at rest [confirm], passwordless sign-in with hashed one-time codes valid for 10 minutes, per-account access isolation, server-side admin checks, rate limits, and server-held service keys. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law, explaining what happened, the likely impact and the steps taken.

08

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access: a summary of your data and of who it is shared with. Most of it is visible in the app, and you can download it from Profile → Privacy.
  • Correction and update: edit minutes and your name in the app, or ask us.
  • Erasure: delete a conversation from Records or your account from Profile. Data we must keep by law, such as invoices, is retained.
  • Withdraw consent: from Profile → Privacy, or by email. This stops further processing but does not affect processing already done.
  • Nominate: name a person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated.
  • Grievance redressal: complain to our Grievance Officer. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

We acknowledge requests within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.
