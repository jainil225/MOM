MOC export update

Word MOCs now come out in a client-ready firm format, transcripts download as Word files, and the PDF download that failed on the live site is fixed.

Effective 30 October 2026All usersNo action needed

How a MOC reaches the client

  1. 1
    CaptureRecord live in the browser or upload an audio or video file.
    User
  2. 2
    TranscribeSpeech becomes text. With diarization, each speaker is identified and named.
    AiX Track
  3. 3
    Generate MOCExecutive summary, action plan, pending tracker, points discussed and next agenda.
    AiX Track
  4. 4
    Quality checkDecisions and actions are checked against the transcript. Unclear items are marked "requires confirmation".
    AiX Track
  5. 5
    Review and editThe user confirms the MOC before it goes out.
    User
  6. 6
    DownloadUPDATEDWord MOC in firm format, transcript in Word, or PDF.
    User
  7. 7
    ShareEmail, WhatsApp or a secure link.
    User

The Word MOC

Page layout

  1. Firm name, service line, Minutes of Conversation, client and date
  2. Key facts: date, time, venue, subject, present, prepared by, status, reference
  3. Executive Summary
  4. Action Plan
  5. Information / Pending Tracker
  6. Points Discussed (3.1, 3.2 …)
  7. Agreed Directions
  8. Current Pending Status
  9. Proposed Agenda for Next Review
  10. Disclaimer and page footer

Client MOC

Confirmed decisions and agreed actions only. This is the version to send out.

Detailed MOC

For internal use. Adds options raised, key figures and the transcript lines behind each decision and action.

Firm details

Firm name, service line and disclaimer come from each user's Profile settings.
